# Visual Identity & Design System: WEBMORPH

## Concept
**Premium Spider-Verse × Developer Infrastructure**

WEBMORPH is a serious developer infrastructure product. The Spider-Verse visual motifs are used to communicate dimensionality, underlying network structures, and kinetic energy, without resorting to cartoonish or comic book fan art.

## DOs
- **Geometry & Grids:** Use angular panels, technical grid backgrounds, and architectural structures.
- **Webs/Threads:** Represent the "web" data through subtle interconnected thread graphics, nodes, or wireframes.
- **Dimensional Layering:** Use drop shadows, glassmorphism, or offset panels to show depth.
- **Restrained Halftone:** Use very subtle halftone dot patterns as background textures or hover states.
- **Subtle Chromatic Offset:** Use slight RGB-split or glitch effects *only* when highlighting errors, drift, or degradation.
- **Kinetic Transitions:** Use sharp, fast, easing transitions (e.g., sliding panels) instead of slow fades.
- **Color Palette:** Deep dark infrastructure mode (slate/obsidian), high-contrast white text. Technical blue/cyan is used for normal/operational states. Electric red is reserved for alerts, drift, healing, and degradation indicators.

## DON'Ts
- **No Characters:** No Spider-Man models, silhouettes, or masks.
- **No Marvel Assets:** No IP infringement.
- **No Fan Art:** Keep it professional and B2B focused.
- **No Cartoon UI:** Do not use comic sans, heavy black cartoon borders, or excessive speech bubbles.
- **No Excessive Neon:** Keep accents sharp but don't make it look like a cyberpunk nightclub.

## Typography
- **Primary:** Inter or Roboto (Clean, modern sans-serif).
- **Monospace:** JetBrains Mono or Fira Code (For JSON, data contracts, and CLI outputs).
- **Display:** A slightly wider, architectural sans-serif for headings (e.g., Space Grotesk) to give a subtle "comic title" kinetic energy without being literal.
